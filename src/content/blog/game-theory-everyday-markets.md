Game theory is often taught with abstract payoff matrices, but its intuition shows up in mundane places: which checkout line to join, when a shop drops its price, how two cafés on the same street avoid a race to the bottom.

## Best responses everywhere

A best response is simply your optimal move given what you expect others to do. Most everyday "strategy" is an informal version of exactly this calculation.

## Equilibrium, not optimum

Nash equilibrium describes where no one wants to move unilaterally — which is not always the outcome everyone would prefer. The prisoner's dilemma is the classic reminder.
