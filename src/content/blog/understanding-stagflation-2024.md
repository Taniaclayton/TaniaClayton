Stagflation — stagnant growth alongside persistent inflation — breaks the usual policy trade-off. The tools that fight one tend to worsen the other, which is why the current climate is so awkward for central banks.

## The supply side

Much of the recent inflation is supply-driven, which monetary tightening addresses only bluntly. Raising rates cools demand but does little for constrained supply chains.

## The policy bind

Fiscal stimulus risks feeding inflation; austerity risks deepening the slowdown. The result is a narrow, uncomfortable path for policymakers.
